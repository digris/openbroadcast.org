General Remarks

This program layout provides the frame for the creative content of our users. It complies with the specifications in the license application and makes a distinction beween working days and the weekend. In terms of a user-generated radio the layout is valid until further developed by the community. A mode for developing it further is still to be found. We are open for suggestions.

User Generated Form of Program: Roles and Responsibilities

  • The producing community respects the rules of collaboration regarding the functions of author and daily editor in chief.
  • Autors produce content: Spoken content, playlists, recordings of DJ sets, livesets and concerts.
  • Editors also produce content, and edit content in their function of daily editor in chief.

Program Guidelines

  • Open Broadcast appreciates the privilege of not having to broadcast mainstream.
  • The demands for editing of the music in the individual dayparts complements the word content designated in that daypart.
  • According to the license application the overall proportionality of word/music should be 30/70.
  • A radio broadcast can last from 15 to 120 minutes. Each broadcast starts with a jingle. About three jingles should be played in one hour.

Production of Spoken Content

Although the interface does not provide the function for uploading word content yet, creative misuse is welcome. Depending on the knowhow and technical equipment spoken content can be produced at home and uploaded into the Music Library as complete broadcast or as files like music tracks.

Program Concept

Monday – Friday

06.00-07.00 Early Bird

  • Mood: The day breaks gently with noises.
  • Music: Sounds and noises of the daybreak from cities, forests, deserts, beaches and backyards come closer.
  • Word: Not spoken. Soundscape with noises only.

07.00-09.00 Sali! (Hi!)

  • Mood: Waking up and leaving, in a hurry.
  • Music: A cheerful morning buzz, witty and lightfooted.
  • Word: Mini formats of knowledge, education, comedy, fine irony.

09.00-12.00 Mikro

  • Mood: Concentration and intellectual curiosity.
  • Music: Musically distinctly different: to give the spoken content more room and relevance the music is exclusively instrumental, of all styles, including game sound and film scores but no dancefloor.
  • Word: Knowledge, science and arts, exact and disciplinary, in reports, interviews and speeches.

12.00-13.00 Politur

  • Mood: News different. The theme of the polyglot noon hour is “higher truth”!
  • Music: Agit pop, polit hiphop – music with socio-critical or political content.
  • Word: News takeover from around the world, or polit comedy/satire.

13.00-16.00 Scope

  • Mood: Interconnection and permeation of disciplines and themes in broad focus: interdisciplinary.
  • Music: Thematic programs of various styles and news. No DJ sets.
  • Word: Arts, knowledge, science, technology, economy and everyday life.

16.00-17.00 Autour du Monde (Around the World)

  • Mood: We’re interested in the global village.
  • Music: The music travels along in the respective styles of the countries and regions around the world. This is world music in the best sense.
  • Word: Planet and people – looks into different living environments.

17.00-20.00 Brot & Spiele (Bread & Circuses)

  • Mood: Offers allure and entertain. Urban culture has lost its innocence. How do fun & games relate to technology, economy and power?
  • Music: House in radio edits.
  • Word: What the city has to offer – culture, culture of consumption and trends – in the form of event calendar, report, column, disputation, review and interview

20.00-23.00 Box & Live

  • Mood: Artists and bohemians with their music, art, passion and expertise – a box full of surprises.
  • Music: Live jam, concert, label show case, DJ set, live broadcast from The Satisfactory. Alternative, experimental, special interest.
  • Word: Commenting: concerts, label showcases, DJ sets, users with friends in exchange over records.

23.00-24.00 Ozean (Ocean)

  • Mood: The day ends relaxed in an ocean of sound.
  • Music: Letting go of the day with mixes, where fragments of songs, sounds and everyday noises intertwine to an “ocean of sound” (see “Ocean of Sound” by David Toop).
  • Word: No spoken features.

24.00-06.00 Nuits Sauvages (Wild Nights)

  • Mood: Every night is the night of nights.
  • Music: Underground, wild style, everything goes.
  • Word: Underground, wild style, everything goes. Trying the impossible.

Saturday

06.00-09.00 Crossfader

  • Mood: Sleepy – chill in and chill out.
  • Music: Musically a challenge because night owls and early birds cross paths here.
  • Word: No spoken features.

09.00-12.00 Radar

  • Mood: Time to listen closely – music special
  • Music: Rarities, sensations, exceptions – if news then under this aspect.
  • Word: Commented rarities, sensations, exceptions.

12.00-17.00 Massaker (Massacre)

  • Mood: Soundtrack and comment on the madness of the shopping rage.
  • Music: Rock music from soft, to Alternative and Hardcore – digestible because diversified.
  • Word: Public space: characteristics of crowds, stress, traffic, public transport – in the form of ironical comment, report, interview.

17.00-21.00 Brot & Spiele (Bread & Circuses)

  • Mood: Offers allure and entertain. Urban culture has lost its innocence. How do fun & games relate to technology, economy and power?
  • Music: House in radio edits.
  • Word: What the city has to offer – culture, culture of consumption, trends – in the form of event calendar, report, column, disputation, review, interview.

21.00-02.00 Live & Floor

  • Mood: Fun with artists and their music, DJ sets, passion and expertise.
  • Music: Live and/or dance with and without guests and friends.
  • Word: Interviews, if any.

02.00-06.00 Tanz der Vampire

  • Mood: A night with bite and sensuality.
  • Music: Dance music.
  • Word: Do vampires talk?

Sunday

06.00-09.00 Crossfader

  • Mood: Sleepy – chill in and chill out.
  • Music: Musically a challenge because night owls and early birds cross paths here.
  • Word: No spoken features.

09.00-12.00 Eiertanz (Walk on Eggshells)

  • Mood: Tender leisure on sunday morning – sitting and lying down.
  • Music: music, that breaks no breakfast eggs: organic, unplugged, sensuous, authentic, not synthetic. Free our folk music from its negative image. Jazz.
  • Word: The music is the message.

12.00-16.00 Fiktion & Wahrheit (Truth & Fiction)

  • Mood: Believing in fiction over the fact.
  • Music: Music with lyrical texts and references to literature. Songs from stage material, and dreampop: floating and bittersweet.
  • Word: Stories, poetry, slam poetry, readings, radio plays and audio books.

16.00-20.00 Hörsturz (Pressure Cooker)

  • Mood: Sunday’s period of grace is over – howl with laughter or being in a rage.
  • Music: Alternative rock, hiphop, music to let off steam.
  • Word: Excerpts from your combox or texts for the Wailing Wall.

20.00-24.00 Krimi (Crime)

  • Mood: The tail light of the week.
  • Music: Music with patina: from the Charleston of the 20ies to music from early gangster- and mafia films, film noir and crime TV series.
  • Word: Crime in film, literature, comics and audio books – in the form of reviews, talk and audio series.

00.00-06.00 Creative Commons

  • Mood: A dive into the big and deep pool of productions under free licenses.
  • Music: Creative commons only.
  • Word: The music is the message.
