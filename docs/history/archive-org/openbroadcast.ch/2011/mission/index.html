Mission Statement

Preamble

  • Open Broadcast is charitable and not-for-profit.
  • Open Broadcast makes the discussion of media-related content publicly accessible on a web-based platform, supporting processes that help shape public opinion.

Vision

  • Open Broadcast creates a public space for the media production of the future.

Mission

  • Open Broadcast provides an internationally relevant web-based platform for scholars and members of the arts and media sector.
  • The Open Broadcast Community produces a high-standard arts and science radio (user-generated content).

Overall strategy

  • Institutions such as universities, art galleries and museums, media companies or event organisers are integrated in the usage and media production of the platform (by means of three-way co-operations, pooling, collaboration or merging).
  • Open Broadcast uses the synergy potential of various types of media.
  • Open Broadcast enables laypersons to collaborate with other authors on optimising the quality of the content delivered by themselves.
  • Open Broadcast makes possible transparent and decentralised decision-making processes in the field of media.
  • Open Broadcast creates added value for its target groups.
  • Open Broadcast guarantees structural openness and flexibility.
  • Digris will legally separate from Open Broadcast in the long term.

Platform Strategy

  • Open Broadcast places the platform at the disposal of other media providers free of charge.
  • The Open Broadcast Platform offers the Community tools for planning and producing media content (Pre-production and Content Module).
  • The Open Broadcast Platform offers the Community tools for broadcasting music and media pieces (radio automation software).
  • The Platform offers a media archive at the disposal of as many users as is legally possible.
  • The Platform is distinguished by straighforward usability, a complete set of meta data, as well as a high level of credibility.
  • The efficient usage of the Open Broadcast Platform and Channel is regulated by rules.
  • Participants choose their own role in the media production process.
  • Open Broadcast is based on the principles of Open Source, Open Content and Open Access.

Radio Strategy

  • The Open Broadcast Channel enhances the relevance of the Open Broadcast Platform.
  • The Open Broadcast Channel is constructing local network hubs in Basle, Zurich and Berne.
  • Signal technology influences radio production formats (DAB, VHF band, analogue and digital cable provide various additional services and multimedia options).
  • The Open Broadcast Channel offers On-demand distribution.
  • The Open Broadcast Community takes on responsibility for their activities and productions.
  • The Open Broadcast Channel encourages listeners to use the Platform and participate in productions.
  • The DAB radio launches with an attractive music programme.
  • Radio events and projects serve to build up the Community, but must largely be funded through partnerships.
  • The production of talk radio is funded independently and preferably for the long term.
